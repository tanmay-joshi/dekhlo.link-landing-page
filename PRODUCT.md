# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, confirmed by the user. No framework or build step.

## Users

Boutique owners who currently photograph garments on a phone and sell or share their collections through Instagram and WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

dekhlo.link turns the product photos a boutique already has into polished, Instagram-worthy posts and a clean collection that is easy to share. Success means a first-time boutique owner understands the before/after value quickly and starts a WhatsApp conversation.

## Positioning

The product transforms ordinary garment photos into presentation-ready social content while keeping the boutique workflow familiar: photos in, a polished collection out, ready for Instagram and WhatsApp.

## Operating Context

Mobile-first use; boutique owners work from phone photos, Instagram, and WhatsApp. The landing page's primary action opens WhatsApp at +91 94138 58007.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The first release is a lightweight static landing page.
- The hero must demonstrate a raw-photo-to-polished-post transformation.
- Generated examples are concepts and must be labelled honestly.
- Product and garment details should be represented truthfully in real customer work.

## Brand Commitments

The name is dekhlo.link. The attached brand kit is the identity reference: friendly, playful, editorial, clear, and helpful rather than corporate or overly technical. Preserve the supplied logo artwork, Ink `#12110F`, Paper `#F7F3E8`, Mint `#4DE6A5`, Lilac `#C8A7FF`, and the open-window framing idea. Voice is warm, direct, short, and may use light Hinglish.
